Go live in 4 steps — under 5 minutes

Works on Keeta Partner App & Web. Single store (SMB) or multi-store brand (KA/SA) — set up per store in your Advertising center.

1Open AdvertisingPartner App / Web
  • Log in to Keeta Partner App or Web.
  • Left menu → Advertising.
  • SMB (one shop): set up in your own store account.
    KA/SA (brand): open the brand account and manage each store.
Advertising entry in Keeta Partner Web (left menu)
2Activate & set budget2 settings only
  • Click Activate (top-right) or Edit campaign.
  • Duration: Run weekly — auto-renews, budget resets every Monday  or  Run a one-off — custom dates, stops at expiry or when budget is used.
  • Budget: start with the system-recommended amount — adjustable anytime.
  • Cost per click (CPC): keep Automatic bidding.
Choose duration → set budget → keep Automatic bidding
3Save & go liveInstant effect
  • Click Save — the campaign takes effect immediately.
  • Your store now ranks higher in the store list, homepage icons & search.
  • The system tracks performance and tunes CPC automatically — no further action needed.
Campaign live: status "Active", budget & duration shown in Campaign settings
4Track & optimiseSame page, T+1
  • On the same Advertising page, check Impressions · Clicks · Orders · Ad earnings · ROAS · Ad cost (Yesterday / Last 7 / Last 30 days).
  • Data updates T+1 — today shows yesterday's numbers.
  • Cost details: Financials → Balance history or Bill download.
Performance dashboard on Keeta Partner App
💡 Good to know
⏸ Pause anytime

Edit campaign → deactivate at the bottom of the page. Your ranking returns to organic right away.

💰 Where does budget come from?

No top-up needed — ad costs are deducted directly from your sales. Any unspent budget returns to your withdrawable account as revenue.

🔄 Budget used up?

Delivery pauses automatically and resumes next Monday — or raise the budget anytime to keep running.

🎟 Vouchers

Ad vouchers offset part of the cost — check the coupon page. Auto-renewal is OFF by default.

🌱 New store?

Allow a 2–3 week learning period with platform traffic support; run promotions alongside for a bigger boost.

That's it — your Advertising is live.

Stuck at any step? Ask your Keeta BD for a walk-through, and we are more than happy to help!
